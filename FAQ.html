FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I create an account?

You can create an account from the login page by clicking Register.

Can I play offline?

Some songs and modes can be played offline after being downloaded.

How do I unlock more songs?

Complete missions, level up, or download new song packs.

Which platforms are supported?

The game is planned for Android, iOS and PC.
